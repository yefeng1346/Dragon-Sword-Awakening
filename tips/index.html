Tips & Practical Fixes

Small decisions that make combat cleaner, progression steadier and Orbis easier to read.

On this page

Combat tipsProgressionExplorationBossesPC & performance

Best first tip

Stop attacking half a second earlier.

Combat

Win the rhythm before the damage race

01

Leave one escape

Do not spend every movement and control option at the start of a pattern.

02

Switch with a reason

Tag for a Signal payoff, a new ailment or a safer position—not just because switching is available.

03

Watch posture

Large effects are distracting. Read the enemy model and animation wind-up before the damage numbers.

04

Learn one chain

A consistent two-step handoff is more valuable than five unreliable combos.

05

Rotate cooldowns

Moving through the party keeps pressure up while individual kits recover.

06

Reset cleanly

If the setup misses, regain spacing instead of forcing the payoff into the wrong state.

★

Community-observed behavior: players report that normal attacks can reduce Active Skill cooldowns and that swapping lets the previous hero finish their current action. Confirm exact behavior in your current version, but use it as a reason to stay active rather than waiting passively.

Progression

Build depth before breadth

Fund the active trio

Keep your story team ready before investing equally across all 19 heroes.

Check the reward path

Quests can grant heroes directly or contribute Nameless Souls. Read the reward panel before leaving content unfinished.

Keep replacement logic

When adding a new hero, preserve the setup → answer relationship that made the old team functional.

Delay perfect farming

Use functional upgrades while the story is still opening systems. Optimize sets once a real progression wall appears.

Exploration

Travel in loops

Choose a main destination, clear discoveries near the route, then return to a hub or story marker. This prevents early exploration from turning into a long detour through content you cannot fully use yet.

  • Look beneath watchtowers for the secret cellars mentioned in official game material.
  • Gather cooking ingredients as you travel instead of farming one item too early.
  • Use Familiars for utility or preference; the free and cosmetic variants do not replace party fundamentals.
  • Mark underwater branches or cave routes you cannot finish and revisit them after story progress.
  • Combine resident requests with nearby main or hero objectives whenever possible.

Bosses

Use the first attempt as research

Observe

Survive one complete cycle

Identify the pause, sound or posture before the largest attacks.

Test

Check the ailment response

Confirm which control states build, trigger or shorten against this target.

Commit

Spend burst in the safe window

Hold the strongest Signal chain for a recovery, stagger or clearly completed pattern.

Diagnose

Upgrade the actual weakness

Time-out means damage or uptime; early defeat means survival, spacing or pattern recognition.

PC & performance

Install on an SSD

Hound13's Steam requirements explicitly warn that an HDD may cause extended loading, texture pop-in, frame drops or unexpected crashes. Storage choice is the first performance fix, not an optional polish step.
