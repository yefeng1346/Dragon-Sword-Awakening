Editorial & Corrections Policy

The rules used to separate official facts, release-build evidence and community observations.

Evidence

Source hierarchy

  1. Current in-game evidence and reproducible release-build testing.
  2. Official Hound13 announcements, patch notes and Steam store information.
  3. Official trailers, livestreams and developer Q&A material.
  4. Credible community reports with visible evidence and version context.
  5. Unverified discussion, used only as a lead and never as the sole basis for a factual claim.

Workflow

Review and version labels

Claims are checked against the most relevant available source. Facts that can change after a patch include a visible review date or version baseline. Hands-on testing is labeled only when a named tester, platform, game version and reproducible steps are documented.

Tier grades, exact damage values, unlock conditions and hidden quest steps are withheld when the evidence is incomplete or conflicts across versions.

Tools

Automation may assist, but it does not become a source

Automation can assist with drafting, formatting and technical checks. Every factual game claim still requires source review. Generated details that cannot be tied to evidence are removed rather than published as plausible filler.

Corrections

How errors are handled

Substantive corrections update the affected page, its visible review date and the sitemap modification date. Minor spelling or layout changes do not receive a false freshness update.

Submit a correction

Send the page URL, the disputed sentence, current game version and a screenshot or primary-source link through the contact page.

Independence

Commercial relationships do not set conclusions

The homepage includes a clearly labeled third-party advertisement, and a Google AdSense publisher script is installed while the account is under review. Advertising revenue does not change guide rankings, recommendations, source selection or correction standards. Sponsored editorial placements are not accepted unless they are explicitly identified.
