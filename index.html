Core mechanic

Combat is a conversation between three heroes

DragonSword is built around Status Ailments, Active Skills and Signal Skills. The party order matters because one hero can create the exact condition another hero needs to enter with a payoff attack.

  1. ApplyBuild a Status Ailment with attacks and Active Skills.
  2. AnswerWatch for a compatible Signal Skill prompt.
  3. SwitchBring in the next hero and keep the chain moving.

The world

A radiant continent with paths above and below ground

Official Steam material confirms meadows, watchtower cellars, treasure-filled deep seas, hidden caves and dungeons. Cooking, resident requests and collectible Familiars all feed the exploration loop.

Editorial policy

New game, careful claims

Launch-week information changes quickly. This guide separates confirmed facts from community observations instead of filling gaps with invented data.

Useful, clearly labeled
