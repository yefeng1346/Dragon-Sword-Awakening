Buying & access

Before you purchase

Is DragonSword: Awakening a gacha game?

No. The Steam release is a premium single-purchase action RPG. Hound13 states that all playable heroes and core content are obtainable through gameplay. Optional DLC includes items such as costumes and Familiars, but the launch roster is not acquired through a paid character gacha.

How much does the game cost?

The U.S. Steam launch price is listed at $29.99 for the base game. Regional pricing, discounts and bundle prices can change, so use the official Steam store as the current authority.

Can it be played offline?

Yes. Official launch guidance says you can play offline as long as you are logged into Steam. Online co-op activities naturally require a connection.

When was DragonSword: Awakening released?

Steam lists July 22, 2026. The worldwide launch announcement used July 23 at 07:00 KST, which is the same launch window expressed in Korea time.

Gameplay & modes

How the game is structured

Is it single-player or multiplayer?

The story and open-world adventure are primarily single-player. The Steam page also lists multiplayer and Online Co-op for supported activities. Developer-based community documentation identifies two-player boss content and three-player raids.

Is DragonSword: Awakening an MMORPG?

No. It is an action RPG centered on story, exploration and a three-hero party. The optional co-op modes do not turn the open world into an MMO.

What is the main combat mechanic?

Heroes build Status Ailments with attacks and Active Skills, then use compatible Signal Skills for a payoff. Switching Signals let party members enter during the sequence and continue the combo.

What activities are in the open world?

Official Steam material confirms meadows, watchtower cellars, deep-sea treasure, hidden caves and dungeons. Cooking, resident requests and collectible Familiar companions are also part of the adventure.

Heroes

Roster and recruitment

How many playable heroes are there?

The launch roster contains 19 playable heroes.

How do I unlock new heroes?

Progress through quests. Hound13 says heroes are either granted directly or assembled using the in-game Nameless Souls material.

Does buying a costume unlock its character?

Do not assume so. Costumes are cosmetic DLC, while heroes are obtained through gameplay. Unlock the hero through the appropriate quest or Nameless Souls path.

Who are the main story characters?

The official story introduction centers on Lute, a young traveler; Johnny, a mercenary; and Castella, an Elf. Lute is pulled into their chaotic mercenary life on the road to Orbis.

PC & languages

Technical questions

Does the game require an SSD?

Yes for the intended experience. Hound13 warns that installing on an HDD may cause extended loading, texture pop-in, frame drops or unexpected crashes.

What are the minimum PC requirements?

Windows 10 64-bit, Intel i5-9400F, 8 GB RAM, GeForce GTX 1660, DirectX 12 and 25 GB of SSD space.

What are the recommended PC requirements?

Windows 10 64-bit, Intel i7-9700F, 16 GB RAM, GeForce RTX 3060, DirectX 12 and 25 GB of SSD space.

Which languages are supported?

At launch, Steam lists interface and subtitles for English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Thai and Brazilian Portuguese. Korean is listed for full audio.

Was generative AI used to create the game assets?

According to the Steam disclosure, generative AI was not used to create in-game content resources. Hound13 says AI was used only for translation of some dialogue and external content such as Steam and community posts.
