Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of data handling on DragonSword Guide.

Policy status

Effective August 2, 2026.

Effective August 2, 2026Advertising Disabled

Overview

What this site collects

DragonSword Guide does not provide user accounts, accept payments or directly request personal information through on-site forms. The site does not currently load advertising scripts or third-party analytics cookies.

Infrastructure

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through Vercel. Hosting providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps and security events to deliver and protect the service. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

External services

Links to other websites

Pages link to Steam, GitHub, Reddit and other external resources. Following an external link makes that provider's privacy practices applicable. DragonSword Guide does not control external cookies, accounts or tracking.

Local behavior

Search and browser storage

The guide search runs in the browser and does not send the search phrase to a site database. The current site code does not intentionally write analytics or advertising cookies. A short cleanup routine unregisters the advertising service worker used by an earlier site version.

Changes & questions

Policy updates

If analytics, advertising, forms or other data-processing features are introduced, this policy will be updated before or alongside that change. Questions can be submitted through the contact page.
