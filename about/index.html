Mission

Useful answers without invented certainty

DragonSword Guide is an independent reference for players of DragonSword: Awakening on Steam.

New-game search results fill quickly with copied tables, speculative tier lists and details that cannot be traced to the release build. This site takes a narrower approach: publish what can be sourced, label community observations, and leave uncertain fields blank until they can be checked.

Publisher

Who maintains this site

The site is maintained by the DragonSword Guide Editorial Team. The public website code and revision history are available in the project repository ↗, providing a visible record of technical and editorial changes.

Current pages are source-reviewed against the official Steam store, Hound13 announcements and clearly identified community material. A source review is not presented as a substitute for hands-on testing; future gameplay tests will be labeled with the tester, platform and game version.

Method

How information is checked

  • Official Steam and Hound13 material is the default source for release facts, modes, pricing and requirements.
  • Release-build evidence is preferred for combat, rewards, routes and unlock conditions.
  • Community reports are labeled and are not promoted to fact without corroboration.
  • Page dates reflect meaningful review or content changes, not automatic daily refreshes.

Full details are published in the editorial and corrections policy.
